Something I need you to know →
for the nights that feel too long

Snow, this feeling is not forever.

I know you've been feeling lonely lately. And when you've felt that way for a while, it's hard to imagine life ever feeling different. I won't ask you to pretend you're okay just because it's your birthday.

But the way life feels right now is not the way the rest of your life has to be. There are people you haven't met, memories you haven't made, and versions of happiness you haven't had the chance to discover yet.

While you're waiting for things to feel lighter, you don't have to wait alone. Come to me with the same thought a hundred times. Be quiet, upset, tired, or have nothing to say at all. You don't have to make your pain easier to understand for me to stay.

You don't have to feel hopeful every day. I'll hold on to a little hope for you on the days you can't.

You're my brother on your good days and on the days when simply making it through is enough. I'm so, so glad you're here.

I wrote you a letter →
a letter from hoor

To my brother.

Dear Snow,

Happy birthday to one of the most precious people in my life. I don't think you realise how much your presence has meant to me. Somewhere along the way, you stopped being just a friend and became the brother I got to choose.

I've seen how understanding you are, how you think about everyone else's feelings, and how you keep such a gentle heart even when life isn't gentle with you. I hope you never mistake that softness for weakness. It's one of the most beautiful things about you.

For your birthday, I wish you the kind of happiness that doesn't make you question whether you deserve it. I wish you people who show up for you the way you show up for everyone else, days that feel lighter, and a hundred little reasons to smile when you least expect them.

I know this birthday might not find you at your happiest, and I wish I could take some of the weight off your heart. I can't tell you exactly when the loneliness will lift. But I can promise you never need to put on a happy face for me. Call me when you have a thousand things to say, and call me when you don't have a single word. We can talk about everything or sit in comfortable silence. Your place in my life isn't something a bad day can take away.

One day, I hope you look back at this chapter and realise it wasn't the end of your happiness. There is still so much life left for you to fall in love with. Until then, we'll take it one day at a time. I'm proud of you for making it here, even when it hasn't been easy.

Thank you for being my Snow. Life is so much better with you in it. Happy birthday, idiot. I love you, brother.

Always in your corner,
Hoor ♡
